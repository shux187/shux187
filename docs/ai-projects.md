## Why this matters

Section 40 of the brief ("No Fabrication Rule") explicitly forbids fabricating AI model
performance or overstating capability. A bio claiming "AI Agents · RAG · LLM APIs" with nothing
to back it up is the exact failure mode that rule exists to prevent — even though it would make
the profile *look* more impressive on first scroll.
